# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis & Churned Customer Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn using **Power BI** to understand overall churn patterns and identify the key characteristics of customers who have already churned.

The project is divided into **two interactive dashboards**:

1. **Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard** – Provides an overall view of customer churn, retention, churn rate, and key factors influencing customer churn.
2. **Churned Customer Analysis Dashboard** – Focuses specifically on customers who have churned to understand their characteristics, behavior, services, contracts, tenure, and payment methods.

The objective is to help businesses identify **high-risk customer segments**, understand the drivers of churn, and develop effective **customer retention strategies**.

---

# 📊 Dashboard 1: Customer Churn Analysis

The **Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard** provides an overall view of the company's customer base and churn performance.

### Key Areas Analyzed

* Total Customers
* Churned Customers
* Retained Customers
* Churn Rate
* Customer demographics
* Contract types
* Internet services
* Payment methods
* Customer tenure
* Factors associated with customer churn

### 🎯 Purpose

This dashboard helps businesses answer questions such as:

* How many customers have churned?
* What is the overall churn rate?
* Which customer segments are more likely to churn?
* Which contract types have higher churn?
* How does tenure affect customer retention?
* What customer characteristics are associated with churn?

---

# 📊 Dashboard 2: Churned Customer Analysis

The **Churned Customer Analysis Dashboard** focuses exclusively on customers who have already churned.

### Key KPIs

| KPI                                 |        Value |
| ----------------------------------- | -----------: |
| Churned Customers                   |        1,869 |
| Average Tenure                      | 17.98 months |
| Average Monthly Charges             |       $74.44 |
| Revenue Lost from Churned Customers |     $139.13K |

### 📈 Key Analyses

#### Churned Customers by Services

Analyzes the services used by churned customers, including:

* Device Protection
* Online Security
* Online Backup
* Tech Support

**Key Finding:** Device Protection has the highest count among the analyzed services, with **545 churned customers**.

#### Churned Customers by Contract

* Month-to-month: **1,655**
* One-year: **166**
* Two-year: **48**

**Key Finding:** Churn is heavily concentrated among **month-to-month customers**.

#### Churned Customers by Internet Service

* Fiber Optic: **69%**
* DSL: **24.54%**
* No Internet Service: **6%**

**Key Finding:** **Fiber optic users represent the largest share of churned customers**.

#### Churned Customers by Tenure Group

* Less than 1 year: **999 customers**
* 5–6 years: **99 customers**

**Key Finding:** Customers with **less than one year of tenure** are the most represented among churned customers.

#### Churned Customers by Payment Method

**Key Finding:** **Electronic Check** is the most common payment method among churned customers, with **1,071 customers**.

---

# 💡 Key Insights

* **Month-to-month contracts drive churn**, accounting for 1,655 of 1,869 churned customers.
* **Early-tenure customers are most vulnerable**, with 999 churned customers having less than one year of tenure.
* **Fiber optic users account for the highest share** of churned customers at 69%.
* **Electronic Check is the most common payment method** among churned customers, with 1,071 customers.
* **Device Protection is the most-used analyzed service** among churned customers, with 545 customers.

### 🔎 Overall Insight

> Churn is concentrated among **early-tenure customers on month-to-month contracts**, particularly those using **fiber optic services** and **electronic check payments**.

---

# 📌 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses can:

1. **Strengthen onboarding and engagement** during the first year of the customer lifecycle.
2. **Encourage month-to-month customers to adopt longer-term contracts** through loyalty benefits and targeted offers.
3. **Investigate customer experience among fiber optic users** to identify potential service-related issues.
4. **Promote convenient payment options**, such as automatic payments, to reduce payment-related friction.
5. **Create targeted retention campaigns** for high-risk customer segments.
6. **Monitor churn indicators regularly** to proactively engage customers before they leave.
